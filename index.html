The Alchemist’s Notes

What each brew can do
❦ ❦ ❦
🌿

Healing DraughtVerdant Moss

Restores health to one ally. A very strong brew can rival, or even beat, a Cleric’s Mend.

🍄

Venom FlaskToadcap

Wounds one enemy and poisons them. Stronger brews poison for longer.

🔥

Fire BombEmber Pepper

Blasts every enemy at once. With 3 or more peppers, it also sets them ablaze.

❄️

Frost PhilterFrost Berry

Hurts one enemy and drains their magic. With 3 or more berries, it also stuns them (bosses need 4).

⚡

Storm TonicSpark Bloom

Charges the whole party with SP. With 3 or more blooms, the next attack also hits 50% harder.

✨

Aegis ElixirStarpetal

Wraps one ally in a protective shield. With 3 or more petals, their parry zone is also doubled for the round.

🫧

Awkward Potiononly 2 of a kind

A fumbled brew. Gives one ally a little of everything: a small heal, a cure for poison, a trickle of SP, and a slight boost to their next hit. Always costs 10 SP.

⚗️

Philosopher’s Draught5 different ingredients

The grandest brew. Heals the whole party, cures poison, restores SP, and makes the next 2 attacks hit 50% harder. Perfect parries banked beforehand make it better still.

On the art of brewingWhichever ingredient fills the most of your bag decides the potion, and the more of it you hold, the stronger the brew (4 alike makes it Potent, 5 makes it Pure). With only 2 of a kind, the brew turns out as a weak Awkward Potion instead. Stronger brews cost more SP to make: 5 SP per level of strength, 10 SP for an Awkward Potion, or a flat 30 SP for the Philosopher’s Draught. Perfect parries bank bonus strength for your next potion, up to a maximum of +3.
